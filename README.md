# Free invoice templates — Word and Excel, seven languages

Blank invoice templates and an invoice ledger, free to use, change and share. **No signup, no watermark, no attribution required** ([CC0 1.0](LICENSE)).

Made by [Spoonbill](https://spoonbill.app), invoicing for freelancers in Europe. If you'd rather not fill a template by hand, the [free invoice generator](https://spoonbill.app/en/invoice-generator) makes the PDF in your browser.

## The templates

Each one comes as `.docx` (Word, Google Docs, LibreOffice) and `.xlsx` (Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers).

| Template | For |
|---|---|
| `invoice-template-basic` | the essentials, one table |
| `invoice-template-itemised` | goods with quantity and unit price |
| `invoice-template-hourly` | hours × rate |
| `invoice-template-service` | a service at a fixed price |
| `invoice-template-freelance` | a project for a client |
| `invoice-template-consulting` | consulting days |
| `invoice-template-deposit` | a deposit before the work |
| `invoice-template-retainer` | a monthly retainer |

## What a valid invoice needs

A template is only the layout. What must be on the invoice depends on your country:

- Germany: [Pflichtangaben auf Rechnungen](https://spoonbill.app/de/guides/countries/invoicing-in-germany)
- Austria: [Rechnungsmerkmale in Österreich](https://spoonbill.app/de/guides/countries/invoicing-in-austria)
- Switzerland: [Rechnung schreiben in der Schweiz](https://spoonbill.app/de/guides/countries/invoicing-in-switzerland)
- Numbering: [invoice numbering](https://spoonbill.app/en/guides/invoice-numbering)
